New-hire onboarding notes
=========================

Day 1:
- Collect laptop and badge from reception.
- Sign in to the SSO portal and enrol MFA.
- Join #general, #it-help, and your team channel.

Day 2:
- Complete the security awareness training module.
- Read the employee handbook (docs://handbook).
- Set up your dev environment from the shared drive.

Useful contacts:
- IT help desk: #it-help
- Security team: security@techcorp.local
